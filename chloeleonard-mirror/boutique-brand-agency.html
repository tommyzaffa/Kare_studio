With nearly a decade of experience shaping small to mid-sized businesses, CL Studio is more than a creative partner. We’re a long-term ally in building brands with staying power.

A BOUTIQUE BRAND AGENCY FOR SMALL BUSINESSES

Based in Nashville and working with brands nationwide, CL Studio is a boutique brand agency for small businesses. Led by Chloe Leonard, we help service-based and e-commerce businesses transform from DIY beginnings into fully realized brands built for confidence and staying power.

As a boutique brand agency focusing on web design, branding, and marketing, we take on a select number of clients to ensure each project receives the attention and intention it deserves. You'll work directly with the seasoned designers, developers and professionals behind the vision — collaborators who are as invested in your brand’s evolution as you are.

We know lasting brands aren’t built on visuals alone. They’re rooted in strategy, shaped by positioning, and brought to life through thoughtful execution. We design cohesive brand ecosystems that fuel long-term growth, aligning identity, strategy, web, and marketing direction across every touchpoint.

The Experience

Meet Your Team

Chloe Leonard

Founder + Creative Director

What started as building pretend brands and websites in collage quickly evolved into a decade-long career spanning photography, editing, and social media marketing for major wellness brands and Sony Music, studying PR in Florence, and ultimately multi-media design.

Since 2017 starting as a solopreneur and now a digital brand agency, she’s brought brands to life for clients like Almost 30 Podcast, Clearstem Skincare, Harper Collins, and Free People, with a focus on SEO-driven strategy, elevated branding, and custom websites that meet clients where they are and help take them where they want to go. Based in Nashville, TN, she splits her time between designing from coffee shops (happily paying $7+ for a great cappuccino), hanging with her dog Bowie, and writing and performing music for her own projects.

Sofia Shackel

Associate Designer

Sofia is a Nashville-based graphic designer and Chloe's right hand woman. With an education in design, and over 4 years of design experience, she knows that every brand deserves a thoughtful approach and purposeful direction to truly stand out. You may see Sofia in all-hands meeting, but most of her work is behind the scenes executing on the finer details that elevate your branding, website, collateral, or templates.

When she’s not designing, Sofia can usually be found curled up on the couch with a book on her Kindle, working out at F45/taking a long walk around the neighborhood, or baking sourdough. She’s rarely without a kombucha, always has jazz music on in the background, and loves to snack on Dot’s Pretzels.
